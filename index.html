About Us

Welcome to the MLMD (Machine Learning & Mechanism Design) Group at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Jilin University (JLU), led by Dr. Changyi Ma. We investigate robust and adaptive machine learning methods for complex intelligent systems, with the goal of bridging fundamental AI research and real-world engineering and design problems. Our research spans Continual Learning, Information Retrieval, AI-driven Optimization and Mechanism Design, LLMs and Intelligent Agents.
